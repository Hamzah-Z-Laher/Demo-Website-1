Good decisions start with books you can rely on. We handle the day-to-day, monthly or bi-monthly, at our offices or at your premises.

  • Bookkeeping, reconciliations and month-ends
  • Management accounts and trial balances
  • Annual financial statements (IFRS)

Whether your company needs an audit, an independent review or neither depends on its Public Interest Score under the Companies Regulations, 2011. We calculate it and tell you what applies.

  • Public Interest Score (PIS) calculations
  • Independent reviews (limited assurance)
  • Financial statement audits
  • Special-purpose audits
  • Compilations
  • Management letters for management and the board
